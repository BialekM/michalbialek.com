Privacy policy

Effective from 13 November 2026

This page explains what data I collect on michalbialek.com, why, how long I keep it and what rights you have. I keep it plain and simple.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Michał Białek, ul. Krzycka 86E, 53-020 Wrocław, Poland ("I", "me"). For anything related to your data, write to contact@michalbialek.com.

2. What data and why

Contact form and e-mail

When you send the form or e-mail me directly, I process your name, e-mail address, optionally your company name and phone number, the message and the topics you selected. I use them to reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal and continue the conversation.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request before entering into a contract), Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering correspondence) and the consent you give in the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Providing the data is voluntary, but I cannot reply without an e-mail address and a short description.

Working together

If we sign a contract, I process the data needed to perform and invoice it under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and data required by tax and accounting law under Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Server logs

The web server automatically records technical information about visits (IP address, date and time, page address, browser type). It is used for security and troubleshooting (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I do not combine it with other data or use it to track visitors.

3. Who receives the data

  • OVH sp. z o.o. (OVHcloud group) – website and e-mail hosting, servers in the European Union.
  • Web3Forms – a service that delivers the form message to my mailbox. The provider may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case the transfer relies on safeguards provided for in the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Google – only if you click "Show Google map" in the contact section. Only then does your browser connect to Google Maps, and Google processes data under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).
  • My accountant and public authorities – only if we work together or the law requires it.

I do not sell data or share it for marketing purposes.

4. How long I keep data

  • Enquiries that did not lead to a project: up to 12 months after the last message, unless you ask me to delete them sooner.
  • Contract data: for the duration of the work and until any claims become time-barred; accounting records for the period required by tax law (usually 5 years from the end of the tax year).
  • Server logs: according to the hosting settings, usually a few weeks.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw your consent at any time, which does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal. Just e-mail contact@michalbialek.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl), or with the authority in your country.

I do not make automated decisions about you or profile you.

6. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising tools. Fonts are served from my own server, not from third-party services. The only thing stored in your browser (localStorage) is your chosen language, so it is shown again on your next visit. You can clear it in your browser settings.

7. Changes

If I change how data is processed, I will update this page and the date at the top.
